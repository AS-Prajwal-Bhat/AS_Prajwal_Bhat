Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 October 2026

This page explains what happens to information when you visit this website or contact me. The site is a personal portfolio run by A S Prajwal Bhat, a student in Bengaluru, India.

What this site collects

Nothing directly. The site has no accounts, no sign-up or contact forms, no comments, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. The light and dark theme switch is not saved anywhere.

What your browser sends anyway

  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page and may keep server logs. GitHub's own privacy statement covers that data.
  • Fonts. The text is set in fonts loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser requests them from Google, which receives your IP address and browser details.

I do not receive or see any of this data.

If you email me

If you write to prajwalbhat113@gmail.com, I receive your email address and whatever you put in the message. I use it only to reply and for any follow-up you ask for. I do not add you to mailing lists, sell your details or share them with anyone. You can ask me to delete your messages at any time.

Links to other sites

This site links to GitHub, LinkedIn, IEEE and other sites. They have their own privacy policies and I am not responsible for them.

Changes

If I change this policy, the new version and its date will appear on this page.
